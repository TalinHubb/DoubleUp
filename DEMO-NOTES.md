# Double Up concept storefront

This is a sales prototype for a future multi-location retail platform. It demonstrates the customer experience without collecting data or promising a production backend.

## Included in the prototype

- Age-confirmation experience
- Eight fictional store locations
- Location-aware sample availability
- Searchable and filterable product catalog
- Persistent device-local pickup bag
- Demo-only pickup request
- Demo-only loyalty enrollment preview
- Manager dashboard concept
- Responsive mobile and desktop layouts
- Social sharing artwork

## Replace before a client presentation

Update `storeLocations` in `products.js` with approved store names, addresses, phone numbers, and hours. Confirm all product categories and terminology with the client. The current addresses, numbers, availability, pricing, and dashboard metrics are fictional.

## Production phase

A real launch would require an inventory/POS integration, server-side pickup handling, an approved loyalty and SMS provider, privacy and consent language, security controls, accessibility testing, and jurisdiction-specific legal review. The age gate by itself is not a compliance system.
